5 - Token Booster (Crystals Spent: 370k)
At this point, it is a good time to consider maxing Token Booster. This does not directly help your progression, but helps you gain tokens quicker for the Juno output token upgrade. Crystal upgrades at this point are getting weaker and weaker, so boosting tokens is not a bad investment. 
6 - Cost Upg Scalers (Expected SE range: 105 - 113; Crystals Spent: 400k)
Also, at this point, it is worth it to invest in Cost Upg Scalers, but it is a large crystal investment to get started (10K crystals) without immediate return (because you have to then buy cost upgrades).


If you have no cost upgrades, buy ~10 levels in Alpha cost first.

7 - Max Juno cost upgrades (Expected SE range: 112 - 124; Crystals Spent: 430k)
Note: When you get to DCMs 82 - 84 (after ZAT 5 at ~SE 112 - 118), the Juno Cost upgrades give a significant boost to these DCMs from extra Juno stability levels. So, it is better to buy Juno cost upgrades to 500 before any other cost upgrades once you have ZAT 5 and have bought the Cost Upg Scalers.  Also, once you can reach Juno stability level 100 in all run types, then Juno cost upgrades are no longer very valuable, so if you aren’t finished with them at that point, you can continue with other cost upgrades and finish Juno cost upgrades after Ixion cost upgrades are done.

Optional - ZAT 8 push upgrades (Expected SE range: 124 - 125)
Note: When you get to SE 124-125 and are pushing for ZAT 8, there are some additional cost upgrades to buy early for boosting Juno/AP (via talent PP 6-1 due to extra levels in Spawn Rate) instead of continuing to follow the rest of the guide.  You need to have both Cost Upg Scalers and then the precedence for Juno/AP boost for any cost upgrades you don’t have maxed is Juno, Delta, Ceti, Beta, Alpha, Gamma, and Fenix.


Optimal strategy here is to buy cost upgrades until the spawn rate level reaches the next level divisible by 100 (e.g. level 2000), and then there is a cost increase gap.  After that, follow the precedence of Juno, Delta, Ceti, Beta, Alpha, Gamma, Fenix for those that are most efficient to get through the cost increase gap.

Cost upgrades notes:


Beta cost upgrades need 50 levels in Beta output first


Note: Beta cost upgrades are better than Alpha cost upgrades because they have more attributes with smaller cost increases per level and include Attraction which boosts Talent PP6-3 (DC).  The actual direct boost to Beta or Alpha output from more attribute levels is a very small factor compared to the boost to row 6 talents.
