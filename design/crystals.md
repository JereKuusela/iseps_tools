### SE10-24

SE10-24 :ISEPS_ResourceCrystals: Crystal Guide:

Complete everything in ⁠👶│early-game-chat crystal guide pin that is not optional.  Once that is done and you have reached player level 90, start saving crystals for level 120 and beyond unlocks.

When Ixion is unlocked, pause saving to buy Tiberius Bundle
 and then go back to saving.

Note that level 120, 130, and 140 Specials are 500 crystals per level and 50 levels each.  This is a total of 75K crystals, so save as much as possible.

At player level 120, spend all saved crystals on level 120 Special, :ISEPS_CorvusExtractions: Bulk Extractions
.

Keep buying level 120 Special until level 130, then start spending on level 130 Special, Bulk Researches
.

Then at level 140, start spending on the level 140 Special, Bulk DC Milestones
.

The goal at this point is to get these 3 specials, abbreviated ERM, to a 1:1:1 ratio. That is, at any point level 120 or later, simply buy whichever of these 3 specials is available to you

### SE25

Save 3000 crystals for level 180 One-Timer, Ultima Bundle
.

After saving 3000 crystals, you can continue buying Bulk Extractions, Bulk Researches, and Bulk DC Milestones (abbreviated ERM) at 1:1:1 ratio.  That is, simply buy whichever of these 3 upgrades is at the lowest level.

At player level 180, buy the level 180 One-Timer, and then save another 3000 crystals for the level 200 One-Timer, Infinity Multi Bundle
.

At player level 200, buy the level 200 One-Timer, and then continue on ERM at 1:1:1.

Once all ERM levels are above 20, then buy all levels of the level 190 Special, Infinity Max Lvl (5 levels - 3000 crystals each)
, then continue on ERM at 1:1:1.

If your Total Emblem Milestones (EMs) shown in Tiberius Colony gets to 50 or more, then you can buy 5 levels of the level 225 Special, Bulk Emblem Milestones (EMs), (cost: 500 crystals for 1st level, increases 50 each level)
.

If you finish everything listed here, then you should buy levels of the level 225 Special, and can also start following the SE 50-100 crystal guide found in ⁠tuntematon.

SE25-49 Crystal priority chart (buy the highest item listed and available to you):

1) Level 180 One-Timer, Ultima Bundle - 3000 crystals

2) Level 200 One-Timer, Infinity Multi Bundle - 3000 crystals

3) Bulk Extractions, Bulk Researches, Bulk DC Milestones (ERM) Special (1:1:1 ratio) to levels 20:20:20
4) Level 190 Special, Infinity Max Lvl - 3000 crystals per level

5) Bulk Extractions, Bulk Researches, Bulk DC Milestones (ERM) Special (1:1:1 ratio) all levels
6) Level 225 Special, Bulk Emblem Milestones (EMs) - 500 crystals for 1st level, increases 50 each level

All new items unlock with Kappa except AP Bundle (MK2) which unlocks at SE 125.  If you're SE 125+, but not at Kappa yet, buy AP Bundle (MK2) as top priority.

These items below are in order of priority and have priority over the SE 100+ crystal guide.

One-Timer: Meltdown Bundle
